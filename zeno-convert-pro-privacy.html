Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026  |  Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains how Zeno Network Labs ("we", "us") handles information in Zeno Convert Pro ("the App"), our media converter, player and document toolkit for Android and Windows.

Local-first media tools: Files selected for ordinary local playback, editing and supported offline conversion are handled on your device. Content is transmitted to a service when you specifically use an online feature such as cloud AI, text translation or server-based Office conversion.
  • Android Free users may see ads; Premium users do not.
  • Account and Premium entitlement use Firebase and Google Play.
  • Cloud features may upload selected content for processing.
  • Local history and settings are stored on your device.

1. Information we process

The App may process the following information according to the features you choose:

  • Files and user content: video, audio, images, subtitles, PDFs, Office documents, pasted text, rich-text drafts and generated outputs that you select or create.
  • Account information: email address, Firebase account identifier and related sign-in or profile information when you create or sign in to an account. Password authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication.
  • Purchases and entitlement: Google Play product identifiers, purchase tokens, verification status, subscription expiry and Premium/Family Premium/Admin entitlement records. We do not receive full payment-card details from Google Play.
  • Local usage and preferences: Android Free export counter, editor drafts, file/output history, playback resume positions, favourites, language and application settings. Some history may contain local file names or paths.
  • Technical information: app/device identifiers, approximate location inferred from IP address, device or operating-system details, advertising interactions, errors and diagnostics that relevant third-party SDKs or service providers may process.

2. Local media and document processing

Normal playback, supported on-device media conversion and locally generated PDFs use the files you choose. The App may create temporary files, thumbnails, drafts and results on your device. Ordinary local operations are not designed to automatically upload your media library to Zeno Network Labs. Files you save, export or share remain wherever you put them.

3. Optional cloud processing

When you request online processing, the App sends the selected information to the service needed to provide that operation:

  • AI enhancement: a selected image or document and processing settings are uploaded to a configured Zeno backend/AI provider for enhancement, denoising or upscaling. Sign-in information may be used to authenticate requests.
  • Text translation: the text you submit is sent through a Firebase Cloud Function to Google Cloud Translation; long text may be sent in chunks.
  • Office conversion: an Office/PDF file selected for a server-based conversion is uploaded to the configured conversion service, which processes it and returns the converted result.

These services require internet access and may be unavailable until configured. Please avoid uploading highly sensitive files unless you are comfortable with server-based processing. Service providers may retain operational logs or metadata under their own policies.

4. Microphone, files and device permissions

If you choose voice typing, the App may request microphone permission and use device speech-recognition services. Spoken audio or its transcript may be processed by the operating system's speech provider, possibly using network services depending on the device. The App uses file pickers, shared-file intents and storage access to work with content you select; you can manage permissions in device settings.

5. Advertising on Android

The Android Free edition may display banner, interstitial or rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may process advertising identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, device/app information, ad interactions, diagnostics and applicable privacy or consent choices as described in their policies. Paid Premium, Family Premium and Admin entitlements are intended to remove in-app advertisements. Windows direct-sale Premium does not display mobile ads.

Where legally required, advertising choices and consent must be presented and managed through applicable platform controls. You can also review and adjust your device's advertising privacy settings.

6. Purchases, Premium and family administration

Google Play handles Android subscription or one-time purchase payments and may provide purchase data needed for secure verification. Zeno Network Labs stores entitlement records associated with the signed-in account in Firebase/Firestore. Approved administrators may grant or revoke Family Premium based on the family member's account email address. Windows direct-sale purchases are handled separately through the official Zeno Network sales channel.

7. Why information is used and shared

Information is processed to deliver the features you request, save local settings or progress, authenticate accounts, verify purchases, administer Premium entitlements, prevent abuse, serve ads to eligible Free users, troubleshoot failures and comply with applicable laws. We do not sell your personal documents or files. Selected content is shared with cloud providers when you activate their processing features, or with other apps/services when you intentionally export or share it.

Relevant providers include Google Firebase, Google Cloud Translation, Google Play Billing, Google AdMob and the infrastructure supporting any configured cloud AI or Office conversion service. Their practices are governed by their respective privacy policies.

8. Data retention and account deletion

Local drafts, history, preferences and output files are retained on your device until you delete them, clear app storage or uninstall the App, subject to the locations in which files were saved. Exported or shared copies must be removed separately from their destination.

Firebase account information and server-side entitlement or purchase-verification records may be retained while the account exists and thereafter where necessary for billing, fraud prevention, security or legal obligations. Cloud providers may retain service logs according to their policies. The precise retention period may differ by service and applicable requirements.

Uninstalling the App does not automatically delete a Firebase account or data already shared with other services. Deleting a Zeno account does not by itself cancel an active Google Play subscription; manage subscriptions in Google Play.

9. Security and international processing

We use platform security controls and HTTPS for configured cloud requests, and keep service credentials on servers where appropriate. No electronic system can be guaranteed entirely secure. Cloud providers may process information on servers outside your country, subject to their terms and applicable safeguards.

10. Children

The App is a general-purpose productivity and media utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has supplied personal information, contact us to request appropriate action.

11. Your choices

You may use available offline functions without uploading files to a cloud converter, avoid optional AI and translation features, manage microphone and file permissions, control advertising preferences where supported, clear local app data, and request account deletion or information about personal data controlled by Zeno Network Labs.
